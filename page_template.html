Wiring details

CT1 measures printer hot only; adapter and sensing branches connect before it. White gaps are insulated crossings; filled bus dots are joins. Body footprints match the 3D top view. Terminal symbols are spaced for clarity, and wire bends are simplified.

DENT sensing leads: A1 → L1 (hot), A2 → L2 (neutral), A3 → N (neutral). PE uses a separate rated conductor.

The three original DENT kit pigtails have different colors; drawing colors are schematic. They stay in guarded terminals and mate with the original long voltage leads. Follow A1/A2/A3 labels and disconnect only when fully de-energized. Terminal preparation, protection and CT scale remain acceptance items.
