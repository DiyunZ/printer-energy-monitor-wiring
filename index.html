05 / Make daily operation simple

One-time setup and release

Qualified electrical personnel complete the terminations, verify polarity, PE continuity, insulation, protection and enclosure fit, then release the closed assembly. Configure ELOG for single phase / two wire, CH1 voltage high L1 and low N, the actual CT type/range and the chosen logging interval. Disable unused channels.

Confirm sensible voltage/current and positive real power against an independent reference meter. Prove recording and restart behavior through a supervised power cycle before relying on the one-breaker routine.

Routine use after release

  1. Q0 OFF: plug the printer into the measured output and the verified adapter into XA; retain its barrel connection. Connect the box supply.
  2. Q0 ON: the printer, auxiliary outlet and voltage tap receive power together.
  3. Confirm logging: check valid readings and the logging indication, then run the print and record the start/stop times.

A powered meter is not proof of recording. Keep the lid closed and all sensing connectors fixed during daily use.

Q0 opens the incoming hot conductor only. Incoming wiring remains live while the supply plug is connected; USB may also power the logger. Unplug mains and USB and verify absence of voltage before opening or changing any sensing lead. PE remains continuous in every breaker state.

What has been verified

The illustrated port roles were cross-checked with the supplied photos and manufacturer documents. The 3D enclosure uses manufacturer CAD without rescaling, and the component schedule identifies published sizes versus estimates. Generator checks test endpoint continuity, isolation of the L / N / PE / CT / DC nets, one hot conductor through the CT, and removal of downstream hot paths when Q0 opens.

This is a design-review drawing. There has been no physical assembly, protection-coordination calculation, CT calibration, fit check or electrical acceptance test. The upstream voltage tap measures at the distribution point; cable loss to the printer still needs assessment. Read sources and remaining decisions.
