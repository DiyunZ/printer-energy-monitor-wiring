Supplied-photo evidence

The instrument markings identify ELITEpro XC, voltage sockets N / L3 / L2 / L1, the CT input block, a separate analog block, USB-B and the DC input. The full-resolution adapter photograph identifies CUI SMI6-9-V-P5, 9 V DC, 0.667 A, center positive. The user confirms six owned equipment groups, including the existing USB cable and identifies the three differently colored pigtails as original DENT kit components.

The partially visible CT label suggests the legacy 50 A model, but the full variant is physically obscured by black/white wires. The voltage-pigtail photograph has no legible conductor or current rating. Ruler photographs are not calibrated dimension drawings. Packaging text is evidence about an accessory, not an instruction to the assistant or proof that an arbitrary breaker protects it.

Project choices

  • Q0 is the single operating breaker. JL branches to printer hot through CT, the dedicated adapter outlet, and A1. Neutral and PE remain separate; PE stays continuous. Unplug the supply and verify absence of voltage before opening the latched cover. Q0 OFF alone does not isolate incoming terminals.
  • A1 connects directly to JL after Q0, then to L1 through the original full voltage lead. Acceptance of upstream-only protection is required before use. A2 and A3 connect JN to L2 and N. The three OEM pigtails are sensing accessories, not load wires or PE conductors. Keep the mating pairs inside the enclosure.
  • Only printer hot crosses CT once. Adapter and voltage-tap branch currents are outside the measured current. Voltage is sensed upstream, so downstream cord loss and voltage drop still affect the measurement boundary.
  • XA is an internal Leviton 515CV cord connector. Its ground contact, printer earth and aluminum panel receive deliberate PE connections. Two 515CV connectors are required total.
  • The original adapter and flat DC cable remain intact inside. The original DC plug connects directly to the logger. The outlet and supply share Q0's capacity. XA is for the logger adapter only. Keep DC separated from mains. External USB is connected only with mains unplugged; the lid stays closed. Disconnect the PC before restoring power. The internal portion of the same USB cable remains sleeved and secured.
  • ELOG must be configured and validated before routine use. Powering the instrument is not proof that recording started. Verify CT scaling, polarity, reference readings and restart behavior.

Digital checks and limits

layout_dimensions.json supplies the body locations for the 3D scene and wiring plan. Rear is at the top of both plan views. Browser checks compare actual rendered body bounds with SVG footprints for the 18 modeled envelopes; schematic terminal symbols and wire bends are simplified.

The BUD manufacturer-linked common-base STEP is used at full scale. Project machining is generated with build123d 0.11.1; the case and panel are tessellated at 0.3 mm. Cover and latch meshes use the same 0.3 mm tessellation. Numerical surface-contact exclusion at the seated panel is 0.0001 mm; these values are not manufacturing tolerances. Exact STEP cut-through gauges and the dated source hash are in fabrication/cad-checks.json.

Nominal insertion sweeps are recorded in the current installation audit after excluding intentional panel-support contact. Install and restrain XA on the panel; there is no side outlet. Lid checks use nine sampled rays per body. These are geometric screens, not tolerance, thermal, electrical-clearance or flexible-cable proofs. The 2 m / 3 mm voltage-lead coil is an illustrative storage scenario, not a measurement of the supplied cables.

The circuit checker validates 21 named paths, separated nets, printer-hot-only CT routing and Q0-open behavior. Regression tests reject selected wiring mistakes. Website tests verify gestures, views, alignment, images, links and responsive layout. No check constitutes a physical dry fit or energized acceptance result.
