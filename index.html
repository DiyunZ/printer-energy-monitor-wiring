Blue sensing leads: A1 → L1 (hot), A2 → L2 (neutral), A3 → N (neutral). PE uses a separate rated conductor.

Connector details

The blue pigtails stay in guarded terminals and connect to the original long voltage leads. Label both ends; keep all three pairs inside the enclosure and disconnect only when fully de-energized. Verify pigtail ratings and CT model before use. The adapter label is confirmed below.

A2 · blueNeutral JN → full voltage lead → L2L2 is connected to neutral in this single-phase topology. A red long lead is still neutral here.

Keep the original flat cord outside; pass only the Tensility 10-02228 round extension through the DC insert.
L3 / CT CH2–CH4 / analog inputsUnused in this one-printer setupNo connections needed. The analog-input terminal block on the USB end is not the CT terminal block.

The XC can also obtain line power through L1–L2; DC power does not replace the voltage-sensing leads. The meter’s S terminal is for a CT shield, not PE. Bond the grounded XA receptacle, steel panel and rail; keep N and PE separate. Keep the original adapter plug and cable intact. See the DENT manual and datasheet.
